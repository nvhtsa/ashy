Locked: Charcoal & Iris

#14121A · #E9E5DD · #9B8AF0 · #6F5BD0 · #89839A — these five tokens are now the brand's only colours, and the full board has been rebuilt around them. The other three stay here as a record: if iris ever feels wrong in six months, swapping it means editing five values, not redesigning anything.

The trade this palette makes: it's the quietest accent of the four on a busy thumbnail, and iris almost disappears against a purple-heavy game UI. That's acceptable because the tag chip's shape carries it there — the accent is a detail you find on a second look, which is the brief.

Test it against real art

Screenshot three of your existing thumbnails and drop the tag chip onto each. If the accent gets lost on one of them, it's too close to that game's palette.

Test it at chip size

Zoom to 15%. If you can still tell the tag is yours, the accent is strong enough. If the whole chip turns into a grey smudge, it isn't.

Only the accent moves

Whatever you pick, the bracket colour is the one token that should never change per series. Neutral field, fixed accent, changing art.
